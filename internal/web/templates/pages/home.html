Manage your team's CPMS webhooks

Use this service to choose which payment events CPMS sends to your team, and where it sends them.

You can:

  • subscribe to events, such as a completed payment or a requested refund
  • set a different destination URL for each environment
  • send a test event to check your endpoint receives it
